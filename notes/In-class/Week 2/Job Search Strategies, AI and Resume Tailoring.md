## Developing an effective job search strategy
- Process
	1. Understanding what you want
	2. Exploring the Labour market
	3. Knowing where to look

## Understanding what you want
- process of introspection and evaluation where you reflect on
	- skills
	- strengths
	- weaknesses
	- interests
	- values
	- experiences
1. Identify technical and soft skills
2. Recognize what skills and experiences you bring the the table
3. Consider what activities, subjects, or tasks you enjoy most
4. Reflect on core values (work-life balance, company culture, opportunities)
5. Envision where you see yourself in a few years
## Exploring the labour market
- understanding the labour market is crucial for crafting a targeted job application
- insights about industry trends, in-demand skills, salary ranges, and key employers
1. Target Opportunities
2. Highlight Relevant Skills
3. Negotiate better
