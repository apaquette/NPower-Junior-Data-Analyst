# Generative AI
- branch of AI that enables software to generate new content
	- natural language dialog
	- images
	- video
	- code
	- other
- ability to generate content is based on a language model
	- trained with huge volumes of data
- encapsulate semantic relationships between language elements
- large language models (LLMs) vs small language models (SLMs)
	- difference is based on the volume of data and variables
	- LLMs are powerful and generalize well, but are costly to train and use
	- SLMs work well in focused scenarios, and cost less
## Scenarios
- implementing chatbots to assist human users
- creating new documents or other content
- automated translation of text
- summarizing or explaining complex documents

# Computer Vision
- accomplished by using a large number of images to train a model
- **Image Classification**: the model is trained with labelled images
- **Object Detection**: the model is trained to identify the location of objects
- **Semantic segmentation**: the model can identify the individual pixels in the image that belong to an object
## Scenarios
- auto-captioning or tag-generation
- visual search
- monitoring stock levels
- identifying items for checkout
- security video monitoring
- authentication through facial recognition
- robotics and self-driving vehicles

# Speech
- **Speech recognition**: ability to "hear" and interpret speech
	- usually takes the form of speech-to-text
- **Speech synthesis**: ability to vocalize words as spoken language
	- usually takes the form of text-to-speech
- AI speech technology is evolving rapidly
	- ignoring background noise
	- detecting interruptions
	- generating increasingly human-like voices
## Scenarios
- Personal AI assistants
- Automated transcription
- Automating audio descriptions of video or text
- Automated speech translation

# Natural language processing
- based on models that are trained to do particular types of text analysis
- many common text analytics use cases where NLP language models can be cost-effective
- Common NLP tasks
	- Entity extraction
	- Text classification
	- Sentiment analysis
	- Language detection

## Scenarios
- Analyzing documents or transcripts of calls and meetings
- Analyzing social media posts, product reviews, or articles
- Implementing chatbots that can answer questions or orchestrate predictable conversational dialogues

# Extract data and insights
- Basis for most document analysis solutions is a computer vision technology called optical character recognition (OCR)
- OCR can identify the location of text in an image, advanced models and interpret individual values
- Most data extraction models have historically focused on extracting fields from text-based forms
- Advanced models can extract information from audio recordings, images, and videos
## Scenarios
- Automated processing of forms and other documents
- Large-scale digitization of data from paper forms
- Indexing documents for search
- Identifying key points and follow-up actions from transcripts or recordings

# Responsible AI
- **Fairness**: substantial risk of data reflecting unconscious bias producing discriminatory outputs
- **Reliability and safety**: based on probabilistic models, and need to take this into account to mitigate risks
- **Privacy and security**: data may include personal information; developers need to ensure training data is kept secure
- **Inclusiveness**: developers should strive to ensure their solutions don't exclude some users
- **Transparency**: Users should be made aware of how the system works and its potential limitations
- **Accountability**: people and organizations that developer AI solutions are accountable for their actions
