# Overview
- Many factors affect an interview process
- Many interviews have first -round, second-round, and final interviews
- Each round has some predictable features to prepare for
# Coding Challenges in Data Analysis
- Coding challenges may be an important part of the interview process
- Coding challenge questions may be complex and will depend on the role
# Final interviewing
- Traditionally done on-site
- Read through your resume and cover letter
- Review the job description
- Study other notes
- Rehearse your elevator pitch
- Plan appropriate appearance
- Use the STAR method to answer questions
- Questions for the interviewer
	- What's the next steps?
	- How long does your recruitment process usually take?
	- What are the primary responsibilities of the position?
	- What would my day-to-day routine look like?
# Interviewing: Discussing a Project
- Emphasize that
	- you understand the project's structure
	- you made reasonable assumptions
	- you handle skills and ideas well
	- you can communicate effectively
- Describe your project
	- provide context
	- Delineate the domain of the project and technical challenge
	- describe the structure
	- explain the advantages and functionality
	- describe tools and technologies
	- organize information by project structure and functional categories
