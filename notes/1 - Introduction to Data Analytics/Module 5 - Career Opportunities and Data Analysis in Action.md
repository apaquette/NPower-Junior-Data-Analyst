## Summary and Highlights

Data Analyst roles are sought after in ever industry, including Banking, Insurance, Healthcare, Retail, or IT.

Currently, the demand for skilled data analysts outweighs the supply, meaning companies are willing to pay a premium to hire analysts.

Data Analyst job roles can be broadly classified as follows:
- Data Analyst Specialist - Start as a Junior Data Analyst and move up to the level of a Principal Analyst by continually advancing your skills from foundational to expert.
- Domain Specialist - For if you have specialisation in a specific domain and want to work your way up to be seen an an authority.
- Analytics-enabled job - Include jobs where having analytic skills can increase your performance and differentiate from your peers.
- Other Data Professions - Including Data Engineer, Big Data Engineer, Data Scientist, Business Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst.

There are several paths you can consider in order to gain entry into the Data Analyst field. These include:
- An academic degree
- Online multi-course specialisations
- Mid-career transition
